Working here

Small team, serious work.

iSecure is a family business, which means no layers of management between you and the people who make decisions, and no being a number in a national roster. Our technicians install and service systems that protect families and some of Australia's best known businesses, and our monitoring operators are the reason the phrase 24/7 means something. If you take pride in doing the job properly, you will fit in.
