Our story

It started with a simple promise.

iSecure began as a family alarm business more than 30 years ago, built on a promise that still runs the company today: do the job properly, charge a fair price, and answer the phone when people need you.

Three decades later we are still Australian owned and still family run from our base in Kingsgrove, Sydney. The tools have changed, from hard-wired sirens to HD cameras you can check from your phone, but the promise has not. We design, install and monitor security for homes, families and businesses across Australia, and we treat every property like it is our own.

Some of Australia's best known brands trust us with their sites. So do thousands of households. We are proud of both in equal measure.
