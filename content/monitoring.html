Why monitoring

An alarm nobody hears is just a noise.

Back-to-base monitoring is the difference between a siren and a response. When your system triggers, the signal reaches our Alert Monitoring Centre instantly. A trained operator assesses it, contacts you or your nominated people, and can notify the police immediately. No waiting for a neighbour to care, no hoping you saw the notification. Someone whose job it is, on it, at 3am.
