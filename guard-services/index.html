Guard services

A guard changes the room.

Some situations need more than cameras and sensors: they need a person at the door with training, judgement and a calm manner. iSecure supplies licensed guards who present well, know your site and act early, with our Alert Monitoring Centre behind them around the clock. One provider for the technology and the people.

Where our guards work

Venues. Retail. Events. Corporate.

Venues and hospitality

Entry management, crowd awareness and a steady presence for hotels, clubs and licensed venues.

Retail

A visible deterrent at the door, support for your staff, and cover for openings, closings and busy trading days.

Events

Entry and crowd management for functions, launches and community events, planned with the organiser beforehand.

Corporate

Front of house and concierge-style security for offices and commercial buildings, professional from the first impression.

A professional guard at the door deters trouble before it starts, reassures your staff and customers, and handles the situations nobody else should have to. Our guards are briefed on your site and your expectations before their first shift.
