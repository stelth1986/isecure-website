Video monitoring

Not just recorded. Watched.

Standard CCTV records what happens. Video monitoring adds people: when an event needs eyes, our operators view your cameras live, verify what is actually happening and act on it. Fewer false alarms, faster responses, and footage that is used the moment it matters, not days later.

Live video verification

Operators view your cameras the moment an event triggers, so responses are based on what is actually happening.

Alarm and camera together

When your alarm triggers, linked cameras confirm the event before anyone is sent, cutting false callouts.

Virtual patrols

Scheduled camera sweeps of your site through the night, without waiting for something to trigger.

Rapid response

Verified events are escalated straight away: you, your contacts, a patrol or the police, per your plan.

Works with your cameras

Most modern CCTV systems can be connected. We confirm compatibility before anything is signed.

Event records

Every monitored event is logged with footage, so you always know what happened and when.
