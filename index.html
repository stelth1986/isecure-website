Alert Monitoring Centre

Someone is always on. That is the whole point.

Our Alert Monitoring Centre operates 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. When your alarm triggers, our team responds instantly and can notify you and the police straight away. That is what back-to-base security means.

01

Trigger

A sensor trips, a panic button is pressed, or a camera flags movement it should not be seeing.

02

Signal

The event reaches our monitoring centre in seconds, around the clock, every day of the year.

03

Respond

We contact you and your nominated contacts and notify the police when it is warranted. Fast response, guaranteed.
