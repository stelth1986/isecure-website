Working here

Small team, serious work.

iSecure is a family business, which means no layers of management between you and the people who make decisions, and no being a number in a national roster. Our technicians install and service systems that protect families and some of Australia's best known businesses, and our monitoring operators are the reason the phrase 24/7 means something. If you take pride in doing the job properly, you will fit in.

Roles we hire for

Where you could fit.

Security technicians

Installing and servicing alarms, CCTV, intercoms and access control across Sydney. NSW security licence and driver's licence required; experience with commercial installs valued.

Monitoring centre operators

Assessing and responding to alarm events around the clock. Calm under pressure, clear on the phone, meticulous with procedure.

Apprentices and juniors

Learn the trade from technicians with decades on the tools. Bring the attitude, we will teach the rest.
