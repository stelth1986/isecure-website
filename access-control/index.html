Why access control

Keys get copied. Credentials get managed.

A lost key means changing locks and hoping. A lost fob means thirty seconds in the system to cancel it. iSecure designs access control that puts you in charge of every door: who opens it, on which days, at what hours, with a record of every entry. It is security that scales from a single office door to a whole site.

Keypads and PIN entry

Code-based entry for doors, gates and garages, with individual codes you can change or cancel any time.

Fobs and swipe cards

Fast, cheap to issue, instantly cancellable when staff change or something goes missing.

Per-door, per-person rules

Give the cleaner weekday evening access and the manager 24/7, without cutting a single key.

Entry records

Know who came in, through which door, and when. Invaluable for incidents and rostering disputes.

Built for business first

Shops, offices, medical rooms, warehouses and body corporates: anywhere staff, contractors and deliveries come and go, access control removes the key problem entirely. We design around how your site actually operates.

And for homes that want it

Gate and garage entry by PIN, no more keys under the mat, and full visibility of comings and goings. Popular with home offices and multi-generation households.
