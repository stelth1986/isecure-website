Detect Alarm Systems

The alarm that never sleeps on the job.

Back-to-base alarm systems with motion, entry and glass break detection, monitored 24/7 by our own Alert Monitoring Centre.

Home/Services/Alarm systems
Why a Detect alarm

Sleep easy. Something is always listening.

A good alarm does three things: it senses the moment something is wrong, it makes staying a very bad idea, and it calls for help without you lifting a finger. iSecure Detect systems are built around commercial grade sensors and our own 24/7 monitoring, so a triggered alarm is never just noise in an empty street.

Motion detection done right

Passive infrared and dual technology sensors that catch genuine movement and ignore false triggers.

Every entry covered

Door and window sensors on the ways in, so the alarm sounds before anyone is inside.

Glass break detection

Acoustic sensors that recognise breaking glass the instant it happens.

Back-to-base, 24/7

Your alarm reports straight to our Alert Monitoring Centre, staffed around the clock. →

Control from your phone

Arm, disarm and check status remotely, and get instant notifications.

Pet friendly options

Sensors configured so the dog does not set off the siren at 3am.

Why invest

An armed, monitored alarm changes the equation.

The Australian Bureau of Statistics recorded around 423,000 break-ins or attempted break-ins in a single year, and around a quarter of affected households experienced repeat attempts. An armed, monitored alarm changes the equation: intruders overwhelmingly pass over properties that fight back.

For homes

Cover the ways in, arm it from bed or from the beach, and know that a 3am trigger wakes our monitoring centre, not just the neighbours.

For business

Protect stock, equipment and premises after hours with zoned systems, staff codes and monitored response. One system can cover offices, back-of-house and warehouse zones separately.
